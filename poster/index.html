Abstract
  • Addresses Technical Hiring Inefficiencies: Solves widespread resume exaggeration, proxy interview cheating, and generic, un-grounded mock interview simulators.
  • Autonomous Multi-Tier Cross-Examination: Deep-parses candidate resumes and inspects live GitHub repositories (AST parsing, commit history) to generate targeted technical inquiries.
  • Live LeetCode-Style DSA Coding Sandbox: Integrates a multi-language (Python, Java, C++, JS) browser-based execution sandbox with automated test case evaluation.
  • Acoustic Speech & Delivery Analytics: Measures words per minute (WPM), speech pacing, and filler word frequency to coach holistic verbal delivery.
  • Zero Question Repetition & Command Control: Employs memory-tracked historical deduplication across 15 senior engineering tracks and cockpit command directives.
  • Google XYZ Resume Enhancement: Delivers post-session feedback with quantifiable metric refactoring suggestions for candidate resumes.
Introduction
  • The Software Hiring Crisis: Over 78% of technology applicants inflate resume claims, while conventional interview loops struggle to assess genuine code comprehension under pressure.
  • Static vs. Dynamic Evaluation: Standard mock interview portals rely on canned question banks, failing to probe failure modes, concurrency, or trade-offs in candidate projects.
  • Ground-Truth Verification: ResumeGrind bridges this gap by automatically fetching candidate GitHub repositories and cross-referencing implementation details against oral answers.
  • Multilingual Accessibility: Delivers technical evaluations in both English and Hindi, democratizing tier-1 tech interview preparation across diverse demographics.
  • Adaptive Interviewer Personas: Dynamically modulates tone between *Friendly Mentor*, *Strict Bar-Raiser*, *Curious Architect*, and *Sarcastic Senior* to replicate authentic engineering pressures.

Objectives & Motivation
  • Eliminate Resume Fraud: Verify candidate authorship by cross-examining actual architecture decisions, edge cases, and code repositories.
  • Multi-Turn Adaptive Drilling: Probe beyond initial high-level claims by challenging concurrency (10k writes/sec), caching stampedes, and outage triage.
  • Integrated Live Problem Solving: Smooth transition from verbal architectural discourse directly into LeetCode algorithm coding without context switching.
  • Speech & Communication Coaching: Quantify acoustic vocal metrics (hesitations, filler words, speech rate) for confidence building.
  • Motivation: Empower engineering students with rigorous, industry-grade interview preparation that mirrors top-tier MAANG and high-scale tech company evaluations.
Experimentation & Benchmark Results
  • Provider Latency: Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite achieved 1.82s response time, enabling fluid conversational dialogue compared to Local Ollama (8.14s).
  • Question Uniqueness: 100% unique follow-up questions across 15 technical domains with zero looping.
  • Algorithmic Triage: Sandbox graded solutions in < 140ms with instant time-complexity feedback.
